## Full description (max 4000)
Eland Athletic is the interval timer for people who train hard and don't want their phone in the way. Big numbers, one tap to start, clear cues when the work changes. No account, no ads, no noise. Just you and the clock.

★ EVERY TIMER YOU NEED
• TABATA: work and rest intervals, any number of rounds
• EMOM: every minute on the minute, or any interval you want
• AMRAP: as many rounds as possible, with a round counter
• FOR TIME: race the clock against a time cap
• COUNTDOWN and STOPWATCH for everything else

★ BUILD YOUR OWN WOD
Chain timers into a full workout: warm-up EMOM, rest, AMRAP finisher. Add notes to each phase ("12 cal row, 8 burpees"), reorder with a drag, and see the whole session at a glance before you start.

★ ALWAYS KNOW WHAT'S NEXT
During the workout a single swipe shows every phase, what you are doing now and what comes next. The phase notes are right there, so you never have to stop and check.

★ CONTROL IT WITHOUT UNLOCKING
On Android 16 the timer becomes a Live Update: round, time and progress in the status bar and on the lock screen, with Pause, Skip and Stop right in the notification. On older versions you get the same controls in a regular notification. The timer keeps running when the screen is off.

★ EVERY SESSION COUNTS
Each finished workout lands in your history. A calendar shows the days you trained, with total sessions, time and your weekly streak. Log your rounds or your time and Eland marks your personal bests.

★ NEVER SKIP A DAY
Set a reminder on any workout: pick the days and the time, and Eland nudges you when it's time to train.

★ FEEL THE CHANGE
Sound and vibration cues for the countdown, every work and rest switch and the end of the workout, so you can keep your eyes on the bar.

★ YOUR DATA, YOUR PHONE
No sign-up and no tracking. Workouts and history stay on your device, and you can export everything to a file and import it on a new phone whenever you like.

★ MADE TO STAY OUT OF THE WAY
Black and orange, large type that reads from across the gym, light or dark theme with six accent colours. English and Italian.

Made for CrossFit, functional training, HIIT, running intervals, calisthenics and any workout where the clock matters.

Stay focused. Stay fast. Stay ELAND.
