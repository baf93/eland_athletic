Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Welcome to Eland Athletic.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Eland Athletic handles your information.

Information Collection

Eland Athletic does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

The app does not require user registration and does NOT collect information such as:

* Name
* Email address
* Phone number
* Location
* Contacts
* Photos
* Health data
* Payment information

Local Storage

Workout settings, timers, and user preferences are stored only on your device.

No data is uploaded to external servers.

Internet Connection

The app does not communicate with any backend service.

Any internet access is only used when the user voluntarily opens external links (for example the GitHub repository or other referenced websites).

Foreground Service

During an active workout, Eland Athletic uses an Android foreground service to keep workout timers running while the app is in the background or when the screen is locked.

This service is used exclusively to provide timer functionality and does not collect or transmit personal information.

Third-Party Services

Eland Athletic does NOT use:

* Analytics services
* Advertising SDKs
* User tracking
* Marketing SDKs

Children’s Privacy

Eland Athletic is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.
